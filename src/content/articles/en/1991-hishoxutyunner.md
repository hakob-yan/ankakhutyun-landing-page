September 21, 1991 is one of the most important days in Armenia's modern history. We spoke with people who voted that day, and with their children, who grew up in an already independent country.

## The parents' stories

Many of the people we spoke with remember the queues at the polling stations and the hope that filled the streets.

## The children's view

For their children, independence is a natural reality. They came to understand its value later, from their parents' stories.
