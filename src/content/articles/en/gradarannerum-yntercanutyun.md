Open reading meetups are starting at the central libraries of Yerevan, Gyumri, and Vanadzor.

## What will we read?

During the first month, we will discuss Armenian literature of the 1990s and how it reflected the reality of the first years of independence.

The meetups are open to everyone, and participation is free.
