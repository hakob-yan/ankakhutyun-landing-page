Every family has stories worth preserving. In this guide, we walk you through a few simple steps.

## Prepare in advance

Make a list of questions, but let the conversation flow freely.

## Record

A phone is enough. What matters is a quiet setting.

## Preserve

Keep the recordings in several places and write down a summary of each one.
