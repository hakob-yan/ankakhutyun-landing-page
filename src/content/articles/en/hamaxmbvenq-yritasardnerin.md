This year, the Generation of Independence initiative is launching a new program to support youth groups working in Armenia's regions.

## Who is the program for?

Groups of young people aged 16 to 30 who have a clear idea for their community can apply.

- educational and cultural projects
- environmental initiatives
- community media and oral history projects

## How to apply

Applications are accepted until December 15. The selected groups will receive not only financial support but also mentorship from our team.

> "We believe that change starts in small communities," says the program coordinator.
